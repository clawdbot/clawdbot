## Diagnostics design

Ordinary materialization failures now project only closed structural fields:

- `stage`: `prepare_directory`, `attachment_write`, or `manifest_write`
- `reason`: a typed `fs_safe_<FsSafeErrorCode>` value, one of three allowlisted errno groups (`permission_denied`, `storage_unavailable`, `target_conflict`), or `unknown`

The current fs-safe non-file collision remains an untyped dependency error, so its exact fixed suffix maps to `target_conflict`. No error path, filename, content, mount destination, or sequential substring replacement participates in diagnostic output construction. Overlapping admitted names therefore cannot influence or partially survive the projection.
