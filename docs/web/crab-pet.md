If you use the [Control UI](/web/control-ui) long enough, at some point a small crab will wander into the bottom of your sidebar, look around, and make itself at home for a few minutes.

This is normal. This is OpenCrustacean.

## What you are looking at

Every session hatches its own crab: its color, size, build, claw proportions, accessories, and personality are rolled fresh each time you load the page. Some are round, some are tall, some have claws that are frankly too big for them. Some nap constantly. Some never sit still. One of them waves a lot.

Hover over a visitor and it will tell you its name.

## When it shows up

Sometimes. That is the point.

The crab is a guest, not furniture. It wanders in when it feels like it, stays a while, and leaves. Some sessions it never shows up at all. There is exactly one situation where it always appears: **when your Gateway disconnects**, the crab comes out and paces, visibly worried, until the connection is back. If the crab looks stressed, check your Gateway.

## Things you can do

- **Hover** to learn its name.
- **Click it** to say hi. It startles, which is rude of you, but it forgives quickly.
- **Click it repeatedly** and you will learn something about crab patience. Keep going and you will learn something about crab dignity.
- **Press and hold** to pet it. There is a heart. Any accumulated grudges are forgotten.
- **Right-click it** to shoo it away for the rest of the page load. It will not take it personally. It will, however, remember.
- **Watch it when a run finishes.** Crabs take genuine pride in your completed work, and it shows when things went well. When things did not go well, they take that seriously too.
- **Move your cursor around.** You are being watched. Affectionately.

## Turning visits off (or back on)

Settings → Appearance → **Crab visits**.

The toggle is browser-local and does exactly what it says. Off means never, including the worried disconnect pacing. Your Gateway status dot continues to work regardless; the crab was never your only source of truth, just the most sympathetic one.

Reduced-motion users get calm, stationary crabs automatically.

Next to it sits **Crab sounds**, which is off by default and stays off until you decide otherwise. Turn it on and the crab becomes very quietly audible when touched. It is a small sound. It is a good sound.

## The Crabdex

Next to the visits toggle lives the **Crabdex**: one silhouette for every coloration known to science, filling in with color as each kind visits you for the first time. It lives entirely in your browser. Nothing is tracked, nothing is synced, nothing is gamified. It is just a shelf.

Most slots fill in on their own over time. A few will take considerably longer. One of them glows.

Hover a filled slot and it will tell you who visited first, and when. The Crabdex never forgets a first meeting, which is more than can be said for most of us.

## Field notes

Collected observations from people who spend too much time watching their sidebar:

- Crabs visit the [dream scene](/concepts/memory) too — the Memory tab on the Agents page — where they are asleep. Observers report the sleeping crab looks suspiciously familiar.
- Night-shift users report their visitors yawn a lot after 10pm.
- Crabs have been seen molting. Witnesses describe the experience as "a lot" and the crab afterwards as "noticeably bigger."
- Some visitors do not come alone.
- Around certain times of the year, crabs have been observed wearing things.
- There is one day each year when every crab looks like it walked out of an old logo. Nobody will tell you which day.
- Not every crab that crosses your sidebar is your crab. Most of them do not even stop.
- At least one recorded visitor was not a crab. It maintains that it is.
- People who have used the same browser for a long time report their crab arrives sooner, stays longer, and waves at them. People who shoo a lot describe a certain distance.
- After a Gateway upgrade, pay attention to what the first visitor is carrying.
- The crab in the sidebar has a cousin in the terminal. It shows up in the `openclaw` banner on days of its own choosing, and no one has successfully predicted which.
- On the days the cousin appears, sidebar crabs have been seen dressed nautically. The two refuse to explain the connection.
- During very long runs, the crab stops playing and settles in to wait with you. Solidarity.
- When something crosses the ledge, the resident stops what it is doing and watches. You would too.
- After bad news, some crabs tidy the ledge. It seems to help them.
- Crabs remember anniversaries even when you do not. If yours shows up in a party hat on an unremarkable day, check the Crabdex for the date you met.
- Devoted hosts report their crabs acquiring titles over the years. There is no known ceremony.
- The dream-scene sleeper is not entirely asleep. Press gently and see.

## Privacy

Everything on this page happens locally in your browser: the randomness, the schedule, the Crabdex. No crab data leaves your machine. OpenCrustacean does not know which crabs you have met, and frankly it is jealous.
