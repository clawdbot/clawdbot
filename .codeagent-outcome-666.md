## Blockers repaired

### Durable delivery adoption

- Managed system events are prepared without removing their in-memory event,
  acknowledging their artifact binding, or tombstoning their durable queue row.
- The recipient user turn stores private `sessionDeliveryAckIds` metadata in
  the durable transcript. Transcript hooks cannot erase this adoption receipt.
- Settlement runs only after transcript admission adopts the turn. It
  acknowledges the artifact binding, acknowledges/tombstones the queue row, and
  then consumes the in-memory event.
- Restart replay enqueue now returns a deferred queue outcome. Successful
  in-memory enqueue no longer causes recovery to tombstone the durable row.
- If a crash occurs after transcript adoption but before settlement, replay
  detects the durable receipt, settles idempotently, and emits no duplicate
  recipient prompt.
- Binding acknowledgement is idempotent and preserves its original
  `delivery_acknowledged_at`.

### Retention lifecycle

- Gateway maintenance now invokes delegate-artifact purge immediately at
  startup and hourly thereafter, with overlap suppression and shutdown cleanup.
- Each purge handles at most 100 expired policies. Repeated runs are
  restart-safe and eventually purge all expired backing bytes.
- Purge preserves policy, recipient outcome, binding, and audit provenance
  while changing claims to `purged` and setting backing bytes to `NULL`.
- Expired inspect, list, and materialize operations fail closed after restart.
  Recipient-local discard remains isolated from other recipients before the
  global retention purge.

### Mixed-session tree proof

- The integration test uses the real host ancestry query with
  `crossSessionTargeting` disabled.
- Only the dispatching session and its host-created causal ancestor are frozen
  into the policy. A sibling and guessed session are excluded.
- Later registry mutation cannot widen the frozen recipient set.
- Later recipient-incarnation drift is rejected against the persisted
  recipient snapshot.

## Independent-review follow-up fixes

### Active-steer durable receipt

- Managed delivery settlement now checks each delivery ID against the message
  actually returned by durable transcript persistence.
- A source-idempotent retry that deduplicates to an older transcript row
  without `sessionDeliveryAckIds` leaves the binding and queue row pending for
  replay.
- A persisted row can acknowledge only the delivery IDs it contains; unrelated
  managed deliveries remain pending.
- The original non-managed adoption callback still runs when no managed
  delivery has durable evidence.

### Ingress finalization ordering

- Frond review found that a fallible managed-delivery settlement ran before the
  original ingress adoption finalizer. After transcript-backed active steering,
  a settlement error could therefore leave the already-injected ingress claim
  untombstoned and replayable.
- The original turn-adoption finalizer now runs first. Managed settlement runs
  afterward and remains recoverable from the durable transcript receipt.
- A focused regression proves that managed settlement failure cannot pre-empt
  ingress finalization, while the settlement error still surfaces.

### Deterministic multi-flow listing

- Listing no longer preflights one unordered acknowledged outcome and then
  applies that historical flow to every binding.
- Every acknowledged recipient binding is evaluated deterministically.
  Available artifacts win over expired, discarded, revoked, corrupt, or
  otherwise unavailable historical claims.
- If no live artifact remains, terminal outcomes use an explicit precedence;
  an expired-only history deterministically returns `expired`.
- Regression coverage creates two independent recipient flows and proves that
  an expired or discarded older flow cannot hide a separate live artifact.
