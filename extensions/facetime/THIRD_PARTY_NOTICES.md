## BlackHole source used for local driver builds

The administrator-approved driver script downloads and compiles BlackHole
v0.7.1 from
<https://github.com/ExistentialAudio/BlackHole/archive/refs/tags/v0.7.1.tar.gz>
and requires SHA-256
`e9de179da54ed55ff27876990f3a2dcfefa66e6bd6cfcba448a8564eabdf3e89`.
