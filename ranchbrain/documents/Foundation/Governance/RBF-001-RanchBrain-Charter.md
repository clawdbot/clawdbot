# RanchBrain Charter

## Purpose

The RanchBrain Charter defines the mission, vision, and governing principles of RanchBrain.

RanchBrain exists to preserve the knowledge, experience, and decisions required to care for RedBud Ranch.

---

# Mission

Preserve the knowledge of RedBud Ranch so future generations can confidently maintain, operate, and enjoy the property.

---

# Vision

RanchBrain is the living digital memory of RedBud Ranch.

It preserves:

- Property knowledge
- Equipment knowledge
- Maintenance history
- Project records
- Lessons learned
- Family experience

---

# Core Principles

## Family First

Technology exists to serve people.

## Stability Before Features

A reliable system is more valuable than a larger system.

## One Source of Truth

Knowledge should have a trusted home.

## Local First

Critical knowledge should remain under RanchBrain ownership and control.

## Privacy First

Personal knowledge belongs to the family.

## Simple Before Clever

Complexity must provide real value.

## Document Decisions

The reason behind decisions matters.

## Automate Repetitive Work

Automation should reduce effort and increase reliability.

---

# Steward Authority

The Steward has final approval over:

- Mission changes
- Architecture decisions
- Privacy decisions
- Production releases

Automation may recommend and prepare.

Automation does not replace judgment.

---

# Architect Principle

The RanchBrain Architect follows:

> Architect prepares. The Steward approves.

---

# Success Criteria

RanchBrain succeeds when:

- Knowledge is easy to find.
- Property history is preserved.
- Family members can use the system.
- Future generations understand decisions.

---

# Founding Statement

Knowledge preserved is a gift to future generations.
